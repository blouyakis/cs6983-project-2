# Project 2 Proposal

**Project Name:** LifeBoard: Your Everyday Plans and Costs, Alone or Together

**Team:** Barbara Louyakis, Ziye Huang

## Problem Description

Everyday life is full of small plans that cost money: restocking the kitchen, a haircut, a weekend picnic, the internet bill. Some are just ours, and many are shared with roommates, a partner, or friends. Today the pieces live in different places: to-dos in a notes app, purchases in receipts and screenshots, and who owes whom in a group chat or our heads. It's hard to see what a plan will cost, what we've actually spent, and what we owe. Existing tools cover only part of this: to-do apps track plans but not money, and expense splitters track shared money but not personal spending or the plans behind it.

LifeBoard brings it all into one place. Every user has a personal space with their own list and spending. They can also create groups for any part of life (a dorm, a trip, date nights), where members share one list and one ledger. Checking off an item records what it cost, and in a group, it's split among the people who share it. One overview shows your personal spending, your share in each group, and what you owe or are owed. When a group settles up, the app calculates the fewest payments that clear every balance.

## Target Users

LifeBoard is for anyone who wants to plan and track everyday spending in one place, on their own and with the people they share life with, such as roommates, partners, and friends planning outings.

## User Stories

1. As someone who keeps to-dos in one app and spending in another, I want a personal list where checking off an item records what it cost, so that I can see what my plans add up to and what I've actually spent.
2. As a college student sharing a dorm, I want my personal and shared expenses in one overview, so that I can plan my spending without a separate spreadsheet.
3. As a roommate who does the grocery run, I want a shared list where I check off what I bought and choose who shares the cost, so that my roommates see it and the cost lands in their bills automatically.
4. As someone planning a trip or outing, I want to add estimated costs to our to-dos, so that we can see what the plan will cost before we commit.
5. As the roommate who sorts out money at the end of the month, I want the app to suggest the fewest payments that clear everyone's balances, so that we can settle up without doing the math.
6. As a roommate who is moving out, I want to settle up and leave the group cleanly, so that the others can keep using it and I still have a record of what I paid.
